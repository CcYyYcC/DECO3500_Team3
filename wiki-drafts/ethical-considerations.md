## 1. Design Context

*Describe the current situation your design intervenes in.*

## 2. Stakeholders

*List everyone with an interest in, or affected by, the design — direct (users, participants, client) and indirect (family, moderators, platform owners, future users).*

## 3. Design Intentions

*State intentions specifically: who this is for, in what context, and for what purpose.*

## 4. Unethical Situations (Speculative)

*Imagine the design in use. What could go wrong? Who could be harmed, excluded, or manipulated?*

## 5. Ethical Scope

*Which of the situations above fall within this project's ethical scope to address? Which are explicitly out of scope, and why?*

## 6. Responsibility Assignment

*For each in-scope situation: what is the team taking responsibility for, and how (design decision, mitigation, disclosure)?*

## 7. Reflection: Ethical Design Thinking in Practice

*Following Loke & Matthews (2020): how did the team's understanding of the ethical dimensions of this project change over time? Where did ethical sensitivity/judgement get exercised during design decisions, testing, or data collection (e.g. interviews)?*
