# 2026-09-08 — Week 7 (studio, Tue + Thu)

**Present:** Yiyang Qin · Bo Wang · Yucheng Chen · Zhaoguo Huang · Yuming Zhong

**State:** Research synthesis consolidated. Concept moved off the screen.

**Discussed / decided:**
- **Conquest Wheel** (讨伐之轮) considered and dropped — the loaded rounds became characters on a
  rotating schedule. Strong narrative and a clear daily ritual, but too many mechanical parts,
  character meanings and rules; the spectacle competed with the reflective choice it was meant
  to support.
- **Design-concepts exploration on the Miro board** worked through **Slow Technology**,
  **Tangible Interaction** and **Speculative Design**. This produced *Tangible Task Blocks* —
  physical blocks moved between Later, Today and Done — the direct ancestor of the Token. Slow
  Technology is where our commitment to gradual, ambient feedback over immediate correction
  came from.
- **Time as a Physical Landscape** selected as the direction: modular blocks for study, gaming,
  rest and social activity, with a planned timeline and an actual timeline built alongside it.
  It treats entertainment and rest as legitimate parts of the day and drops the punitive
  vocabulary.
- Our own **"Responsibility Score"** speculative exercise — a score visible to friends — was
  deliberately uncomfortable, and showed us the line we do not want to cross. **No leaderboard,
  no scores.**
- Two references fixed the form: *In Time* (2011) for the gesture and the tangible container,
  with its economic framing rejected; *Daily Stack* (2010, CIID) for the legibility of a stack
  read across a room.
- Repository reorganised to match the Assignment 3 requirements.

**Blockers:**
- Physical Landscape is too large and has too many loose parts to build in the time available.
