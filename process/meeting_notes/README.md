## How we meet

| When | Where | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday, during class | In person, studio | Quick sync on what moved since Thursday, and anything that needs the tutor |
| Thursday, during class | In person, studio | Working session. The week's deliverable, done together instead of reported on |
| Once a week | Online | Direction, decisions, and dividing up the next week |

If you cannot make one, say so in advance instead of on the day. The rest of the team plans the
session around who is there and what they were bringing.
