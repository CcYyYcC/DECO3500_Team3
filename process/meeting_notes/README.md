# Meeting Notes

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`meetings.md`](meetings.md) | Internal team meeting records |

## How we meet

| When | Where | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday, during class | In person, studio | Quick sync on what moved since Thursday, and anything that needs the tutor |
| Thursday, during class | In person, studio | Working session — the week's deliverable, done together rather than reported on |
| Once a week | Online | Direction, decisions, and dividing up the next week |

If you cannot make one, say so in advance rather than on the day. The rest of the team plans
the session around who is there and what they were bringing.

Ongoing stand-up records live on the
[Team Meetings wiki page](https://github.com/CcYyYcC/DECO3500_Team3/wiki/Team-Meetings),
per the assignment brief; this folder holds the repo-side record.
