## What it needs to cover

A viewer who has never seen the project should come away knowing what it is for, how a
Token is used, and what the Tower does with the result. One complete loop — choose, track,
transfer, read — is worth more than a tour of the hardware.

If the demonstration uses accelerated timing, say so on screen. Eight seconds standing in
for an hour is honest only if it is labelled.
