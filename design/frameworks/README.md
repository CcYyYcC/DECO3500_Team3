# Frameworks

Design theory the project was tested against, and what each one changed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`Miro_Design_Concepts.md`](Miro_Design_Concepts.md) | Miro activity record, 8 September 2026 — Slow Technology, Tangible Interaction, Speculative Design |

Three concepts were explored and each left a mark on the design:

**Slow Technology** (Hallnäs & Redström, 2001) is why the Tower accumulates quietly instead
of alerting. Meaning is meant to develop over repeated encounters rather than arrive as a
notification.

**Tangible Interaction** (Ishii & Ullmer, 1997) is what moved the interaction off the screen.
Its *Tangible Task Blocks* outcome — physical blocks moved between Later, Today and Done — is
the direct ancestor of the Token.

**Speculative Design** (Dunne & Raby, 2013) produced the *Responsibility Score*: an
intentionally uncomfortable future in which a player's real-life responsibility score is
visible to friends. We built it to find the line we did not want to cross, and it is the
reason there is no leaderboard.
