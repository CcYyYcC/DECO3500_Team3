# Design Sprints

Two rounds of a Design Sprint structure: Map → Lightning Demos → Crazy 8s → Solution Sketch
→ Storyboard → Prototype → Scorecard.

| Round | Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Gamified deadline visualisation — the user as a surfer, deadlines as sharks closing in | Abandoned |
| Sprint 2 | Tangible time tokens and a shared tower | Current direction |

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `06-time-tokens-and-tower.jpg` | Sprint 2's converged output — the reduced, buildable system |

Sprint 1's divergent concepts are in [`../initial_concepts/`](../initial_concepts/README.md);
the concept frameworks that informed the shift are in
[`../frameworks/`](../frameworks/Miro_Design_Concepts.md).

**Why Sprint 1 was abandoned**, in short: nothing in it answered how progress on a real task
would reach the system without the user reporting it by hand; a pursuing threat adds pressure
at exactly the moment research said people reach for games; and its social layer amounted to
watching someone else's anxiety. Full account on the
[Design Iterations wiki page](https://github.com/CcYyYcC/DECO3500_Team3/wiki/Design-Iterations).
