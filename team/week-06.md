# 2026-09-01 — Week 6 (studio, Tue + Thu)

**Present:** Yiyang Qin · Bo Wang · Yucheng Chen · Zhaoguo Huang · Yuming Zhong

**State:** Working from proposal feedback. Turning the research into a requirements draft.

**Discussed / decided:**
- **Deadline Shark abandoned.** Three reasons, recorded so we do not revisit this: it depends
  on measuring real-world task progress, and every answer we tried reduced to the user
  reporting by hand — the self-reporting burden the tracking apps in our interviews already
  fail at. A pursuing threat is a pressure device, and our research says people reach for games
  when pressure rises, so it pushes hardest in the wrong direction. And its social layer
  amounted to watching someone else's deadlines close in, which is shared anxiety rather than
  shared awareness.
- **Revolver commitment** explored as the successor. It changes the unit of measurement: the
  user declares how much effort they are willing to commit that day, in discrete units, rather
  than the system estimating task value. The mechanism is worth keeping; the weapon metaphor
  is not.
- Two refined story cards selected as the ones that do the real work — **Story 1** (reduced
  playtime does not mean the problem is resolved) and **Story 5** (the same engagement means
  different things in different lives). Together: *time is not the central variable, priority
  judgement is.*
- Weekly plan and a task issue template added to the repo; GitHub Projects set up.
