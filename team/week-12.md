# 2026-10-20 — Week 12 (studio, Tue + Thu) — **A3b Tradeshow**

**Present:** full team

**State:** Tradeshow week. The build is finished and the repository holds the development
notebook, Round 2 evidence and build photographs. Work this week was exhibition preparation
and finishing the assessed documentation.

## What we did

**Prepared for the exhibition.** The stall had to run with visitors using the prototype
unassisted, so the week went on making that possible: the Tower legend printed and mounted,
spare cables and power ready, a reset procedure between visitors, and a full test run before
the stall opened.

**Printed and produced the presentation material.** The A2 poster was finalised and sent to
print, along with the brochure. The three-minute pitch was prepared, with everyone able to
explain the project rather than one person presenting it.

**Finished the wiki and the assessed documentation.** The remaining wiki pages were completed
and rewritten against the current build instead of the version they were originally written
for. The Design Process Overview and Ethical Considerations were brought to a state where they
could be exported as the submission PDF. The repository was tidied so the evidence behind each
page can actually be found.

## Blockers

**Visitors use the prototype unassisted.** The brief requires it, and both evaluation rounds
say people cannot read the display without someone explaining it. The Tower legend is the
cheapest mitigation and was already in the backlog from Round 1's E2.

**Test B was still unrun.** If no pair could be scheduled before the exhibition, the stall had
to state that real paired interaction remains unverified.

## Next steps

1. Legend mounted and the stall tested end to end before it opens.
2. Poster and brochure printed; pitch ready for any member to deliver.
3. Design Process Overview and Ethical Considerations finished and exported.
4. Repository URL submitted with the wiki accessible to teaching staff.
5. Stall notes kept during the exhibition: what visitors ask and what they misread is free
   evaluation data.

**Due Monday 26 October, 3pm (Week 13):** repository URL submitted to Blackboard with the wiki
accessible to staff, and the PDF export of the Design Process Overview and Ethical
Considerations pages as `GG_3DesignPrototype.pdf`.
