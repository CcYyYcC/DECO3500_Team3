# 2026-08-18 — Week 4 (studio, Tue + Thu)

**Present:** Yiyang Qin · Bo Wang · Yucheng Chen · Zhaoguo Huang · Yuming Zhong

**State:** Interviews run and analysis underway. Preparing the A2 Design Proposal for next week.

**Discussed / decided:**
- **The MOBA-only questionnaire was abandoned.** We expected ranked matches, team dependence
  and continuous rounds to give a distinctive account of problematic play. The same
  experiences turned up in role-playing games, sandbox games, casual games and online
  communities, and we could not justify why MOBA should be the only genre in scope.
- Replaced with a **genre-agnostic, participant-led** interview: warm-up, motivation, decision
  points, conflict, counter-examples, design prompts. Deliberately includes people who play
  rarely or not at all — those negative cases are what stop us assuming gaming behaviour has
  one universal meaning.
- Fourteen interviews completed across the five of us. Aim is contrasting accounts that can
  break our own assumptions, not prevalence.
- **Design opportunity reframed:** gaming is not inherently the problem. The focus moves off
  controlling play time and onto helping people plan their own time.
- Repository created and the file structure agreed.
