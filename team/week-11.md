## Discussed and decided

**The wiki structure was reopened.** We had been working on the assumption that the brief
prescribed a fixed structure. Re-reading it during the meeting showed the required content
could be arranged in a clearer sequence. We agreed the Design Process Overview would open with
the problem and design opportunity, then move through three areas: literature research,
concept development, and prototype development with evaluation. Individual contributions,
limitations and what we learned from evaluation close it. Some page links and navigation
controls were also easy to miss and needed fixing.

**Images belong inside the documentation pages, not on the homepage.** This cleared up a
misunderstanding. A supporting image goes next to the explanation it supports, on the Design
Process Overview or the design iteration pages. The homepage carries a product image or the
finished poster. Videos are linked from the documentation rather than embedded. Images collect
in the repository figure folder and videos go to Teams. We select the images that explain the
development instead of uploading the whole set, and the existing collection of roughly 21
process images does not cover the later stages, so more recent build evidence is needed.
Chinese text in figures has to be removed or adapted for the English presentation.

**Each section needs checking by whoever did the work.** The compiled summaries were written
from shared material by someone who did not know every contributor's detail, so they carry
omissions and descriptions that do not match what actually happened. The design iteration
account was the clearest case: it jumps from an early concept to the latest version, when
intermediate material exists and should be in there. Literature research, interview material
and the protocol all need the same check. We also agreed the compiled summaries were sometimes
too general, and settled on collecting enough detail to work from, condensing the important
points into the overview, and keeping fuller evidence in linked documents.

**Two pages are the submission priority.** The Design Process Overview and Ethical
Considerations are the pages exported as PDFs. The overview runs to about 1,000 words plus
images, as understood in the meeting. It has to explain how the prototype developed, what
changed through iteration, and what user evaluation taught us, with reflection and limitations
alongside the process description. Links carry the reader to the full protocol, prototype
instructions, poster and supporting evidence so the overview itself stays readable.

**Our meeting records are thin and need reconstructing.** The Week 9 stand-up is a separate
record from ongoing internal meetings. Most earlier coordination happened in the WeChat group
and was never written up. We discussed rebuilding the earlier weeks from recollection and from
the development material we still have. One of us had already organised material by project
stage because exact dates were uncertain. **A verified week by week timeline was not
established in this meeting.**

**The next evaluation round was still in preparation.** The arrangement discussed gives each
player three Tokens for study, exercise and gaming. The draft testing material was to be
shared with the group that evening and reviewed afterwards, with a reminder that it needs to
address the social and mobile directions discussed in Week 8. No participant numbers, findings
or completion date were set.

**Integration sequence agreed.** Structure first, then contributions, then a shared review
before anything is published. Members prepare their sections as Markdown and send them to the
group; one of us consolidates and uploads. The team checks the draft together instead of
leaving content decisions to whoever is compiling.
