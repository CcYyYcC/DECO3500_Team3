## What belongs here

The affinity mapping and theme development that turned fourteen interviews into findings
F1–F4. The findings themselves are written up on the
[User Research wiki page](https://github.com/CcYyYcC/DECO3500_Team3/wiki/User-Research); what
is missing is the working that produced them.

- Affinity map — photographs or Miro exports of the clustering
- Theme development — how clusters became F1–F4, and which quotes sit under each
- Anything discarded: clusters that did not hold up are evidence of analysis, not failure

Analysis done on the Miro board should be exported to a file here rather than left as a link,
since board access is not guaranteed after the course.
