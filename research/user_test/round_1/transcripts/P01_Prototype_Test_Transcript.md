# English translation

**Interviewer** Imagine you are about to study for an hour. Please use the blue token.

**Participant** Do I just flip it over?

**Interviewer** What do you think the token is showing now?

**Participant** That I have spent an hour doing this activity.

**Interviewer** How do you know that hour has ended?

**Participant** The number of dots has stopped increasing.

**Interviewer** You mean the dots on the token?

**Participant** Yes.

**Interviewer** What do you think the tower is showing now?

**Participant** The activity corresponding to this colour has accumulated.

**Interviewer** Now please record another hour.

**Participant** It's really hard to get this to register.

**Interviewer** Was there anything about the process that confused you?

**Participant** Nothing really confused me, apart from the fact that it isn't very responsive.

**Interviewer** After flipping the token, what do you think its display represents?

**Participant** On this token, the dots appear one by one to show time passing.

**Interviewer** How do you tell whether it has started or finished?

**Participant** I watch the dots to see whether new ones appear one by one.

**Interviewer** After the first and second attempts, what do you think the tower displayed?

**Participant** It shows that I've done some things. For example, right now I have three hours of sleep time.

**Interviewer** If you could change one thing, what would you change?

**Participant** I'd make it more responsive.

**Interviewer** How do you feel about showing your time like this? For example, if this is in a shared area, the section beside yours would show your roommate's time.

**Participant** I'd definitely only flip the study token. There's no way I'd flip it when I'm gaming. I might pretend to study and get him to keep recording study too.

**Interviewer** What effect do you think it would have if your roommate's study time were displayed next to yours?

**Participant** I don't care what he does. I just don't want him to know what I'm doing.

**Interviewer** What if this device were placed in your own room?

**Participant** That would be quite good: it could record what I did that day. But if I had to use the same device as someone else, I'd feel differently about it.

**Interviewer** Would you want to see the other person's activity?

**Participant** If he were gaming all the time, I'd think that was fine. If he were studying too, I'd find it “annoying”.



# CN中文原话整理版

**采访者** 现在假设你开始学习一个小时，请使用蓝色 Token。

**参与者** 就是把它翻过来吗。

**采访者** 你现在觉得这个 Token 显示的是什么？

**参与者** 就是我做这个事情达到了一个小时。

**采访者** 你怎么知道这一个小时结束了？

**参与者** 就是它这个点数没有再增加了。

**采访者** 你是指这个 Token 的点数？

**参与者** 对。

**采访者** 那你觉得现在 Tower 上显示了什么？

**参与者** 我对应这个颜色的行为累加了。

**采访者** 现在请再记录一个小时的时间。

**参与者** 这个被识别实在是有点难。

**采访者** 你觉得这个过程中有什么让你困惑的？

**参与者** 没有什么困惑的地方，除了它很不灵敏。

**采访者** 翻转 Token 之后，你认为它显示的内容代表什么？

**参与者** 这个 Token 就是一个点一个点地表示时间在流逝。

**采访者** 你是怎么判断它已经开始或者结束的？

**参与者** 看它的点，有没有一个一个地出现新的点。

**采访者** 那第一次和第二次操作之后，你觉得 Tower 上显示了什么？

**参与者** 就是显示我干了一些事情比如我现在有三个小时的睡觉时间。

**采访者** 如果你希望修改一个地方，你觉得会修改什么？

**参与者** 把它变得灵敏一些。

**采访者** 你觉得这样展示你的时间有什么感受？比如这个一部分放在公共区域，一部分是你的旁边这一部分是你室友的。

**参与者** 我肯定只翻学习的，我打游戏不可能翻，我可能会假装学习让他也一直翻学习。

**采访者** 那你觉得你旁边室友的学习情况展示出来，会有什么影响？

**参与者** 我不管他。反正我不能让他知道我在干什么。

**采访者** 那如果这个东西放在你自己的房间呢？

**参与者** 那这是一个挺好的，可以记录我这一天干什么。但是如果我要和别人在同一个装置上操作那感受就变了。

**采访者** 那你觉得你想看别人的状态吗？

**参与者** 我觉得他要是一直在打游戏我觉得挺好的如果他也在学习我觉得“挺烦的”。
