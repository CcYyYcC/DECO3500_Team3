### 05  Exact Times and Recording Activities Shorter Than One Hour

*Original transcription: paragraphs 44–54*

**P02-041 | Participant comment (attribution unverified):** Also, it would be good if the exact time could be recorded when a block drops down.

**P02-042 | Researcher:** Someone earlier felt that not recording exact times was better. Why would you prefer exact times to be recorded?

**P02-043 | Participant comment (attribution unverified):** If I were someone who followed a plan every day, I could use the exact times recorded by the blocks to see whether I had completed things as planned. For example, was I earlier or later than planned, and by how much? That might prompt me to think about the reasons.

**P02-044 | Participant comment (attribution unverified):** But your system only drops a block after a full hour. Activities do not always last exactly one hour. For example, if I play games for an hour and a half, what happens to the remaining half-hour? Flipping it back would not make a block drop either.

**P02-045 | Researcher:** That is worth considering, and we may add a related feature later. The current design records activities that people choose to track during the day, rather than requiring them to record all of their time.

**P02-046 | Participant comment (attribution unverified):** Alternatively, all three shapes could be made up of six dots, with one dot dropping every ten minutes. Then, if you only recorded 50 minutes, the final display would contain five dots rather than all six.
