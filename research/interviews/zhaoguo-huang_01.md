### 1. Do you play digital games regularly? What games do you usually play, and which game have you been playing the most recently?

**Answer:**

Yes. I mainly play games such as *League of Legends* and *Honor of Kings*, usually around two or three times a week. These are the main games I currently spend time playing.

------

### 2. What do you enjoy most about this game, and why is it important or enjoyable to you?

**Answer:**

I think getting information and communicating with teammates are important parts of the game because you need to understand what your teammates are doing and coordinate with them. The most enjoyable part for me is playing with friends and winning games together. When I play with friends, it is not only about the game itself. The social interaction also makes the experience more enjoyable.

------

### 3. Can you walk me through the last time you played this game, from when you decided to start until you stopped?

**Answer:**

I normally play with friends and rarely play alone. We usually play together for a while, and when my friends decide to stop, I normally stop as well because I do not find playing alone very enjoyable.

**Answer:**

I cannot really think of a situation like that. I normally do not keep saying “one more game” after I have already decided to stop. If I feel tired or have something else that I need to do, I normally stop playing.

### 5. Think of a time when you planned to stop playing and do something else, but did not follow through. What happened at the time?

**Answer:**

I cannot really think of an example. Usually, if I have something else that I need to do, I will stop playing. I do not often find myself continuing to play when I already have another activity planned.

### 6. Do you usually play alone, with other people, or interact with others around the game? How does this affect your experience?

**Answer:**

I strongly prefer playing with friends or people I know, and I rarely play alone. Playing with people I know is more enjoyable because we are not only playing the game but also talking, communicating and cooperating with each other. For me, the social experience is an important part of gaming. Playing alone is much less interesting.

------

### 7. Do other people ever influence when you start playing, keep playing, or stop playing? Can you give me an example?

**Answer:**

Yes, especially when it comes to stopping. If the friends I am playing with decide to stop, I normally stop as well instead of continuing on my own. I do this because playing alone is not very enjoyable for me. Once the social part of the experience ends, I have much less motivation to continue playing.

------

### 8. Can you tell me about a time when gaming affected something else you had planned to do, such as sleep, study, work, exercise, or spending time with others?

**Answer:**

I cannot think of any major examples. Gaming generally does not interfere with important things that I have planned. If I need to study, work or do something else, I normally stop playing rather than changing my plans so that I can continue gaming.

------

### 9. Have you ever tried to control or change how much time you spend gaming? What did you try, and how well did it work?

**Answer:**

I have not really used any strict methods such as timers or apps to control my gaming time. I normally manage it based on how I feel and what else I need to do. If I feel tired, I stop playing, and if I have something else to do, I simply do not play or stop the current session. For me, it is more of a natural form of self-control than a formal time-management strategy.

------

### 10. Has there ever been a period when you deliberately cut back or stopped playing a game? What prompted that decision, and what happened afterward?

**Answer:**

Yes. There have been times when I became less interested in MOBA games because they started to feel repetitive or boring. The experience of being matched with teammates who did not play or cooperate well also reduced my enjoyment. I also felt that the games had a relatively low barrier to entry. As the game became less interesting to me, I naturally started playing less or stopped for a period of time.
