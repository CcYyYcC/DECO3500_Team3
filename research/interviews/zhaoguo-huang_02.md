### 6. Do you usually play alone, with other people, or interact with others around the game? How does this affect your experience?

**Answer:**

If my classmates or friends are available, I usually prefer playing with them. If nobody is available, I can still play by myself.

Sometimes, while playing, I also meet random teammates who I get along with. We might add each other as friends and continue playing together for a while, but those relationships usually do not last very long. We may play together for one or two days and then stop contacting each other.

Playing with real-life friends is definitely more enjoyable. When I play alone, I sometimes become less engaged or play more negatively. When I play with friends, that happens much less because we talk, interact and enjoy the game together.

### 7. Do other people ever influence when you start playing, keep playing, or stop playing? Can you give me an example?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely.

For example, I might originally plan to start playing at 3 p.m., but before I start, I might ask a friend whether they want to play together. If they tell me that they are busy and can only play at 5 p.m., I might decide not to play at 3 p.m. and wait until 5 p.m. instead.

So other people can directly influence not only my gaming experience, but also when I decide to start playing.

### 8. Can you tell me about a time when gaming affected something else you had planned to do, such as sleep, study, work, exercise, or spending time with others?

**Answer:**

Yes. Sometimes when I become very absorbed in a game, I lose interest in the other things I originally planned to do. I may feel that I want to enjoy the game properly first before doing anything else.

For example, I might originally plan to go to the gym that day, but if I become too absorbed in the game or feel frustrated and want to keep playing, I may decide not to go to the gym and continue gaming instead.

So when I get deeply involved in a gaming session, it can replace activities that I had already planned.

### 9. Have you ever tried to control or change how much time you spend gaming? What did you try, and how well did it work?

**Answer:**

Yes, I have tried to control my gaming time before, but usually I can only keep it up for a few days.

Once I become very interested in the game again, it becomes difficult to control it purely through self-discipline. I feel that relying only on your own willpower can be quite difficult, and sometimes you may need some kind of external factor or outside support to actually reduce your gaming time.

### 10. Has there ever been a period when you deliberately cut back or stopped playing a game? What prompted that decision, and what happened afterward?

**Answer:**

Yes. At the moment, I barely play games anymore. I have probably gone around two or three months without really playing.

One reason is that I mainly enjoyed competitive games, but recently I have been losing more often. As I have gotten older, I feel that my reaction speed and mechanical skills are not as good as they used to be, so constantly losing makes the game less enjoyable.

Another reason is that there are more things happening in my real life now. My friends also have their own responsibilities, so we do not have as much time to play together. Playing alone is not as enjoyable for me, so naturally I started playing less.

However, I have not completely stopped engaging with gaming content. Instead of playing games myself, I now spend more time watching game livestreams. In a way, I have shifted from actively playing games to watching other people play.
