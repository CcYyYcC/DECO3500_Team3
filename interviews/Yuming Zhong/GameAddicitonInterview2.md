# Gaming Experience Interview

## Opening Statement

We are trying to understand people's real experiences playing video games, and the relationship between gaming and daily life. There are no right or wrong answers here — we're mainly interested in your personal experiences. If possible, please try to share some specific examples.

## Interview Questions

**1. Do you play video games regularly? What games do you usually play? Which one have you been playing the most recently?**

Before I moved here, I used to play video games pretty regularly. Mainly League of Legends, as well as Overwatch, and a lot of single-player Steam games. The one I played the most during that time was League of Legends.

**2. What do you like most about this game? Why is this part important or appealing to you?**

For League of Legends, I really love the skins and visual effects — playing it feels like a visual treat. I also really enjoy the cooperative aspect, working together with others to defeat the other team. It gives me a kind of exciting feeling of intellectual competition, mainly because you're playing against real people.

**3. Can you walk me through your most recent experience playing this game, from when you decided to start playing to when you stopped?**

The most recent time was probably before I moved here. From deciding to start playing to when I finally stopped, I'd usually squeeze in some free time to play, and then stop once it was getting close to when I needed to do the things I was supposed to do. I think it was a fairly enjoyable experience overall.

**4. Can you think of a recent time when you planned to stop but ended up playing much longer than intended? What happened?**

I think this happens to me pretty often. For example, back when I was supposed to be working on job prep or my portfolio, I'd tell myself I'd just play one or two games, and it would often spiral into losing an entire afternoon.

**5. Think back to a time when you planned to stop gaming to do something else, but didn't follow through. What happened?**

Let me think... I haven't really been playing much lately. I remember one time I wanted to stop playing late at night, but I was playing ARAM at the time. I kept thinking "I'll win one more game and then go to sleep," but I ended up losing, and then losing again, and I got this feeling of not wanting to accept it, so I kept pushing myself to keep playing. I think it was mainly this "just one more win" mentality that kept me from stopping in time.

**6. Do you usually play alone, play with others, or interact with other players outside the game? How do these situations affect your gaming experience?**

I usually play alone — if I have free time, I'll log on and play a few rounds of ARAM. Playing with others is a different kind of experience, but it's not really my main reason for playing League of Legends. That said, I do enjoy playing with a group more, though it's not always possible to get that chance. I also really enjoy being able to carry my teammates and fight together as a team — I think it's a great way to strengthen friendships.

**7. Do other people influence when you start, continue, or stop playing? Can you give a specific example?**

Taking my college years as an example, my roommates definitely influenced when I'd start, continue, or stop playing. When one person wanted to log on, a lot of the others in our dorm would usually want to join in too. And when one person logged off, if the others still wanted to keep playing, we could keep going.

**8. Can you tell me about a time when gaming affected other things you had planned to do — like sleeping, studying, working, exercising, or spending time with others?**

This is similar to question 5 — I think especially at night, it affects my sleep. If my computer is right there, I can't resist opening it up to play a few rounds. I think this is a pretty common issue for me, but I don't see it as necessarily a bad thing, because as long as it doesn't drag on too late, it's usually not a big deal.

**9. Have you ever tried to control or change your gaming time? What methods did you try, and how effective were they?**

I have tried to change my gaming habits. The most effective method was honestly just uninstalling the game entirely. Also, especially for online games like League of Legends, you really need good internet — so I'd put myself in an environment with worse internet access. For example, during my junior and senior year of college, I moved somewhere where I could only connect to the campus Wi-Fi, which wasn't very good. In a way, that did curb some of my motivation to play, because the constant lag affecting my gaming experience and causing losses was a really frustrating experience.

**10. Have you ever gone through a period of intentionally cutting back or completely stopping playing a game? What prompted that decision, and what happened afterward?**

Actually, I'm currently in a phase of having completely stopped playing games. What prompted this decision is that I feel like there are more important things in my life right now — I'm currently studying abroad, and I need to prepare and polish my portfolio, as well as keep up with coursework. So I've essentially stopped completely. At first there was definitely a sense of discomfort, but now I feel like gaming isn't as important to me anymore. It used to feel like an inseparable part of my life, but now I think it's fine — because there are more things enriching my life now, like rock climbing and running. I also think just living abroad itself is an interesting experience in its own right, so I don't feel bored enough that I need to play games.
